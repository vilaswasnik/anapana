# Anapana Guided Meditation Scripts

Collection of guided meditation scripts for Anapana practice, suitable for self-reading or recording.

## Script 1: Beginner Session (15 minutes)

### Introduction (1 minute)
*"Welcome to this Anapana meditation practice. Find a comfortable seated position with your back straight but not rigid. You can sit on a chair with your feet flat on the floor, or cross-legged on a cushion - whatever feels most stable and comfortable for you."*

*"Gently close your eyes, or if you prefer, soften your gaze downward. Take a moment to settle into your body and let go of any tension you might be carrying."*

### Initial Settling (2 minutes)
*"Let's begin by taking three conscious breaths together. Breathe in slowly through your nose... and breathe out gently through your nose. Again, breathe in deeply... and release completely. One more time, a full breath in... and let it go naturally."*

*"Now allow your breathing to return to its natural rhythm. There's nothing you need to do to control or change your breath. Simply let your body breathe as it wants to breathe."*

*"Take a moment to scan through your body briefly. Notice your head... your shoulders... your arms... your back... your chest... your abdomen... your legs. If you find any areas of tension, simply acknowledge them with kindness and allow them to soften naturally."*

### Finding the Breath (2 minutes)  
*"Now bring your attention to the area around your nostrils - at the entrance to your nose where the breath naturally enters and leaves your body. This will be your point of focus throughout this practice."*

*"Notice the sensations of breathing at this area. You might feel the touch of air as it enters... perhaps a sense of coolness as you breathe in... and warmth as you breathe out. Or you might notice pressure, tingling, or other subtle sensations. Whatever you experience is perfectly fine."*

*"If you can't feel much at first, that's completely normal. Simply rest your attention in this area and allow the sensations to become apparent in their own time."*

### Main Practice (8 minutes)
*"Continue to observe the breath at the nostrils. Notice how each breath is unique - some are deeper, some are shallower. Some are longer, others shorter. There's no need to make your breathing special or different. Simply observe whatever breath comes naturally."*

*[Pause for 30 seconds]*

*"When you notice that your mind has wandered - and it will wander, this is completely natural - simply return your attention gently to the breath at your nostrils. There's no need to judge yourself or feel frustrated. Each time you notice the mind has wandered and return to the breath, you're strengthening your mindfulness."*

*[Pause for 1 minute]*

*"Continue observing the breath sensations. If thoughts arise, acknowledge them briefly and return to the breath. If sounds occur around you, let them be in the background while keeping your primary attention on the breathing."*

*[Pause for 1 minute]*

*"Notice the quality of coolness as air enters... and warmth as it leaves. Some breaths may be very subtle, others more obvious. Stay present with whatever is happening right now at the entrance to your nostrils."*

*[Pause for 1.5 minutes]*

*"If you find your attention has wandered to planning, remembering, or thinking about something else, that's perfectly okay. Gently return to observing the breath. This returning is not a mistake - it's the essence of the practice."*

*[Pause for 1.5 minutes]*

*"Continue with patience and gentleness. Each breath is a new opportunity to be present. Let the breath be your anchor in this moment."*

*[Pause for 1.5 minutes]*

### Conclusion (2 minutes)
*"As we prepare to conclude this session, continue observing a few more breaths at the nostrils."*

*[Pause for 30 seconds]*

*"Take a moment to appreciate yourself for taking this time to practice meditation. This commitment to your inner development is a gift you give not only to yourself, but to all those around you."*

*"Gradually begin to wiggle your fingers and toes. Take your time - there's no rush. When you're ready, gently open your eyes and take a moment to transition back to your daily activities."*

*"Carry this sense of mindful awareness with you as you continue with your day. Remember, you can return to observing your breath at any time as a source of calm and centeredness."*

## Script 2: Intermediate Session (25 minutes)

### Opening (2 minutes)
*"Welcome to this 25-minute Anapana meditation. Take your meditation posture - alert yet relaxed, with your spine naturally erect. Close your eyes and begin to gather your attention inward."*

*"Notice the transition from the busyness of daily life into this time of inner stillness. Allow your mind to slow down and your body to settle. There's nowhere else you need to be right now."*

*"Take three deeper breaths to signal the beginning of your practice... breathing in awareness... breathing out any tension or preoccupation with the day."*

### Establishing Mindfulness (3 minutes)
*"Allow your breathing to find its natural rhythm. Bring your attention to the familiar place at the entrance of your nostrils where you'll observe the breath throughout this session."*

*"Begin to notice the sensations of breathing in this area. Perhaps feeling the air as it touches the nostrils... the temperature differences between inhalation and exhalation... or subtle vibrations and tingling sensations."*

*"If the sensations seem faint, have patience. Simply maintain your attention in this area and allow the awareness to deepen naturally. There's no force needed - just gentle, sustained attention."*

### Deepening Concentration (18 minutes)
*"Continue observing the breath at the nostrils. Each time you breathe in, notice the qualities of that inhalation. Each time you breathe out, observe the qualities of the exhalation."*

*[Pause for 2 minutes]*

*"When the mind moves away from the breath - to thoughts, sounds, body sensations, or emotions - notice this movement with kindness and gently escort your attention back to the breath at the nostrils."*

*[Pause for 2 minutes]*

*"Some sessions the mind may be quite active, others more calm. Both are fine. Your task is simply to continue returning to the breath with patience and consistency."*

*[Pause for 3 minutes]*

*"Notice if you can observe more subtle aspects of the breathing - perhaps the very beginning of each inhalation... the transition from in-breath to out-breath... the gentle pause at the end of each exhalation."*

*[Pause for 3 minutes]*

*"If difficult emotions or restlessness arise, use the breath as your refuge. You don't need to fight or change these states - simply return your attention to the breath and let them be present without engaging them."*

*[Pause for 3 minutes]*

*"Continue with steady awareness. Each moment of mindfulness of breathing is valuable, regardless of how clear or stable your attention feels. Trust in the process."*

*[Pause for 3 minutes]*

*"As this session continues, see if you can maintain an even gentler quality of attention - alert but not tense, focused but not forced. Let the breath reveal itself to your peaceful awareness."*

*[Pause for 2 minutes]*

### Closing (2 minutes)
*"Begin to bring this meditation session toward a close. Continue observing the breath for a few more moments, appreciating the stillness and awareness you've cultivated."*

*"Reflect briefly on the benefit of this practice - not only for your own peace and wellbeing, but for your capacity to respond to life with greater wisdom and compassion."*

*"Slowly begin to move your fingers and toes. Take your time returning to normal awareness. When you feel ready, gently open your eyes."*

*"Carry the mindfulness and equanimity you've developed in this practice into the activities that follow."*

## Script 3: Advanced Session (45 minutes)

### Beginning (3 minutes)
*"Welcome to this extended Anapana practice. As you settle into your meditation posture, take a moment to appreciate this opportunity for deeper inner development."*

*"Close your eyes and establish the intention to maintain continuous mindfulness of breathing throughout this session. This is a time for cultivating profound concentration and insight."*

*"Begin with several conscious breaths, using each exhalation to release any mental or physical tension. Let each inhalation bring clarity and presence."*

### Establishing Base (7 minutes)
*"Locate the breath sensations at the entrance of your nostrils. This area will be your exclusive focus for the entire session. Commit fully to maintaining attention here."*

*"Begin observing the natural breathing process. Notice the sensations as they arise and pass away moment by moment. Each breath is impermanent, changing, never exactly the same."*

*[Pause for 2 minutes]*

*"When distractions arise, recognize them quickly and return to the breath without delay. In this longer session, swift return to the object of meditation is essential for developing deeper concentration."*

*[Pause for 2 minutes]*

*"Allow your attention to become more refined and precise. Rather than observing breathing in general, focus on the most distinct sensations at the nostrils."*

*[Pause for 2 minutes]*

### Deepening Concentration (32 minutes)
*"Continue with unwavering attention to the breath. Let go of any concern about the length of this session and simply be present with each breath as it comes."*

*[Pause for 4 minutes]*

*"Notice the subtle changes in each breathing cycle. The breath is never static - it's constantly changing in intensity, temperature, duration, and quality. Observe this flowing nature of breathing."*

*[Pause for 4 minutes]*

*"If your concentration becomes very stable, you may notice that the breath sensations become more subtle, refined, even blissful. Whatever arises, maintain equanimity - neither grasping after pleasant states nor pushing away unpleasant ones."*

*[Pause for 4 minutes]*

*"Continue with patient persistence. Some periods may feel easy and focused, others more challenging. Both are part of the natural rhythm of practice. Keep returning to the breath with consistency."*

*[Pause for 4 minutes]*

*"Let your awareness become increasingly subtle and refined. You may begin to notice very fine vibrations, pulsations, or other delicate sensations in the nostril area. Observe whatever appears with equanimity."*

*[Pause for 4 minutes]*

*"If thoughts or emotions arise, let them pass through your awareness like clouds moving through the sky. Your attention remains grounded in the breath, unshaken by whatever mental phenomena appear."*

*[Pause for 4 minutes]*

*"Maintain continuous, moment-to-moment awareness of breathing. This sustained attention is developing both concentration and insight - the ability to see the true nature of all phenomena."*

*[Pause for 4 minutes]*

*"Continue with dedication and patience. Each moment of mindfulness is contributing to your spiritual development and the cultivation of wisdom."*

*[Pause for 4 minutes]*

### Integration and Closing (3 minutes)
*"As this extended session draws to a close, maintain full attention to the breath for these final moments. Honor the commitment you've made to this practice."*

*"Take a moment to dedicate the merit of this practice to the wellbeing and liberation of all beings. May the peace and wisdom you've cultivated benefit not only yourself but all of life."*

*"Very gradually begin to move your body gently. Take plenty of time to transition back to ordinary awareness. When you're ready, slowly open your eyes and remain seated quietly for a few moments."*

## Quick 5-Minute Script

*"Sit comfortably and close your eyes. Take three deep breaths to settle in."*

*[Pause 30 seconds]*

*"Focus your attention at the entrance of your nostrils. Notice the sensations of breathing - cool air in, warm air out."*

*[Pause 2 minutes]*

*"When your mind wanders, gently return to the breath. This returning is the heart of the practice."*

*[Pause 2 minutes]*

*"Take a moment of gratitude for this time of mindfulness. Wiggle your fingers and toes, then slowly open your eyes."*

## Tips for Using These Scripts

### For Personal Practice:
- Read through the script beforehand to familiarize yourself
- You can skip the spoken parts and use the pause intervals as guidance
- Adjust timing to fit your available practice time

### For Recording:
- Speak slowly and clearly with a calm, steady voice
- Leave the suggested pause times or adjust to your preference  
- Consider adding gentle background music or nature sounds
- Test the recording length to ensure it matches your intention

### For Group Leading:
- Practice reading the script aloud several times before leading
- Watch the group's energy and adjust pace accordingly
- Be comfortable with silence during the pause periods
- End with time for questions or brief sharing if appropriate

---

*Remember: These scripts are guides to support your practice. The most important element is your sincere commitment to observing the breath with patience and continuity. Adapt the language and timing to best serve your meditation practice.*
